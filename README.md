## About

I am a third-year Computer Science student at the **Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, Babeș-Bolyai University (Cluj-Napoca)**.

My main interests are **backend development, full-stack systems, and software architecture**, with a focus on understanding how real software systems are designed and implemented.

I am also particularly interested in **databases and optimisation**, especially in the context of designing efficient systems and managing large amounts of structured data.

Beyond this, I have a growing interest in **computer networks and cybersecurity**. While these are areas I am still actively learning, I find them fascinating because they reveal how systems communicate, scale, and remain secure in real-world environments.

Some repositories include **video demonstrations** that show how the applications work in practice.
